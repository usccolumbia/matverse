# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-01

Initial MatVerse survey with 115 dataset/release records in 12 categories and 27 navigation resources. Added descending category tables, dataset evidence cards, a Scientific Data journal index, the requested ACS Perspective crosswalk, a narrative survey, machine-readable JSON/CSV, source/count receipts, and deterministic catalog validation.

Five sizes remain unverified and unranked. Counts are release-specific and heterogeneous; the catalog intentionally omits an additive total of materials. Reported licensing and access uncertainties remain explicit.
