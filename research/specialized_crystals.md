## Counts that require special care

C2DB’s release table lists 16,789 rows, while its introductory paragraph still says about 4,000. Full downloads are currently provided upon request. The official dated JARVIS 2D archive independently contains 1,103 records; its registry says 1,109. The official 2DMatPedia JSONL independently contains 6,351 parseable records.

CoRE MOF 2025 v1.0 reports 43,439 records across access tiers and includes both computation-ready and rejected structures. The openly deposited SI subset contains 9,256 structures, including 2,737 computation-ready and 6,519 rejected entries. The deposit marks the CSD branches as not updated; their availability requires checking. The 2024 release had 40,837 family records and 8,300 SI structures. ASR/FSR variants and the recommended screening list need explicit treatment. MOSAEC-DB similarly separates its open descriptors from CIFs requiring a CSD license.

The current QMOF README supports 20,000+ structures. Its historical 20,375 count is not asserted as a current exact total, because later releases removed structures. ARC-MOF retains a rounded 280,000 summary after removal revisions. The CoRE COF v7 archive independently contains 1,242 CIF files. CURATED COFs is recorded at its July 2022 release of 871 valid structures, excluding 110 discarded entries. ReDD-COFFEE contains 268,687 hypothetical COFs and has CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 file rights.

OC20 is ranked by its corrected released S2EF training set of 133,934,018 configuration frames. The full author paper separately describes 1,281,040 relaxations and approximately 264.89 million single-point evaluations across its wider corpus. OC22 is ranked by approximately 9,854,504 configuration frames; its final author paper reports 62,331 relaxations, while older summaries say 62,521. OC25 reports 7,801,261 calculations across 1,511,270 unique explicit-solvent environments. These environment counts are not counts of unique crystalline substrates.

ODAC23 contains 38,983,748 single points from 176,032 relaxations of 8,412 pristine/defective frameworks. ODAC25 is a nearly 70-million-frame successor with changed reference and sampling conventions. They should remain separate releases. The official current ODAC23 overview contains smaller rounded figures that differ from the original full corpus; this audit uses the original paper’s Table 1.

The public OMC25 card gives 24,870,226 training and 1,386,816 validation frames, totaling 26,257,042 frames from 218,841 starting crystals. Its Scientific Data paper describes a larger 27-million-plus full corpus. The released split total and full study total should not be silently interchanged. OE62 and OMol25 contain isolated molecules and are adjacent resources, not extra periodic crystal datasets.

DigCat remains a navigation resource: its provider reports more than 0.4 million experimental and 0.4 million computational records; its 2024 author preprint reported more than 400,000 performance records and 300,000 structures. The requested ACS Perspective’s claim of more than 500,000 computational records was not independently tied to an exact provider release. The SAC primary paper independently verifies 1,197 configurations, with 657 passing its binding-energy screen; downloadable periodic coordinates and a separate data license remain unverified.

## Licensing, overlap and access

A paper’s open-access license and a repository’s software license were not automatically applied to raw data. Explicit data terms remain unverified for some 2DHub releases, legacy hMOF/PCOD/IZA, X23/ICE13, BEAST and the SAC portal. QMOF, CoRE, ARC-MOF, Boyd-Woo, hMOF and MOSAEC reuse source structures or preprocessing variants; neither their material counts nor their learning labels are additive. IZA counts framework topology types, whereas Zeo-1, OMC and Open Catalyst frame counts include many configurations of each system.

The original BEAST NREL hostname did not resolve from this environment. The paper establishes a public 2024 corpus with more than 20,000 electrochemical calculations, but a current live total and license were not verified. JARVIS surface/interface endpoints returned empty responses during download checks and were not added as separate records.

## Evidence retained

The JSON records cite primary providers, author repositories, archives and primary papers. the published `research/provenance/evidence-specialized-source_receipts.json` preserves URLs, response sizes, SHA-256 hashes and access failures. `count_audit.json` records independent payload counts and arithmetic. `license_audit.json` records verified terms and remaining gaps. Full response bytes remain in a local cache outside the publication files; the repository publishes hash/count receipts instead of complete third-party documents.

Useful future additions requiring further verification include LitMOF-DB, OC20NEB/AQCat25, zeolite adsorption configurations, MOF stability benchmarks, and private molecular-crystal prediction corpora. Broader Alexandria/MC3D/NOMAD families are handled by the root audit. No Git mutation or publication was performed.
