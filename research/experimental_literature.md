## Interpretation and access notes

- No massive data archives were downloaded; structure/record totals are provider- or author-reported unless explicitly identified as arithmetic sums.
- Dataset counts use heterogeneous units and are not comparable as unique-material totals; overlapping databases and derivatives must not be summed.
- Current primary count/licence unavailable for AMCSD and RRUFF; count left null and licence Not verified.
- CSD count is the arithmetic sum of official January-2026 published-structure statistics; AtomWork count historical 2020; AtomWork-Adv count dated March 2026; HTEM count historical 2018.
- ICSD total includes theoretical structures; ICDD includes mixed diffraction provenance; 3DSC labels/structures can be approximate matches.
- Starrydata full and per-project totals overlap; raw sample rows are not deduplicated compositions. See archived manifest for project-specific counts.
- HTEM hostname did not resolve; several portals had retrieval/TLS failures, recorded without bypassing authentication.
- Digital Hydrogen-S portal certificate and publisher access prevented primary-content verification; listed as a resource with no numerical claim.
- Many literature-property resources include noncrystalline samples or polymers; users must filter to their scientific scope.
- Commercial/restricted and freely browsable services are distinguished from permissively licensed bulk data. Not verified is not a claim of unrestricted use.

Specific corrections preserved in the structured records:

- ICSD: 347,985 is a whole-database total, including a theoretical collection. [Provider scope](https://icsd.products.fiz-karlsruhe.de/en/about/about-icsd).
- ICDD: PDF-5+ 2026 headline total is a reference-entry count, and atomic coordinates cover a subset. The [licensing page](https://www.icdd.com/licensing-process/) restricts derivative ML/AI works unless expressly authorized.
- 3DSC: matched structure counts differ from SuperCon-label counts; ICSD CIFs require licensed reconstruction and artificial doping can produce approximate proxy structures. [Descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02721-y).
- Solid-state recipe release v3: 19,488 is the deposited Data Records count; the paper also contains a 19,744 reaction figure elsewhere. The release choice is explicit.
- Impurity-aware synthesis: absence of a reported impurity phase does not prove a phase-pure sample.
- Thermoelectric ChemDataExtractor: Figshare specifies MIT for its data/source archive; that was retained instead of substituting the paper licence.
- DigBat: June-2026 provider figures separate 3,816 experimental electrolyte materials and 27,645 conductivity entries from 852 computational materials. [Tohoku announcement](https://www.tohoku.ac.jp/en/press/digbat_an_ai_ready_digital_platform_for_solid_state_battery_research.html).

- [Scientific Data materials Data Descriptors](https://www.nature.com/sdata/collections?page=3&type=collection) (journal_collection): Journal descriptors are discovery/provenance documents; linked external repositories hold the actual data.
