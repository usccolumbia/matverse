## Evidence and interpretation

Matbench has 13 tasks from 10 source datasets. The structure cohorts above group the paired electronic and elasticity tasks, while the perovskite row explicitly links the CDVAE and Matbench reuse. Task counts are not unique materials totals.

WBM’s 256,963 cleaned entries and 215,488 unique-prototype subset refer to overlapping evaluation views. Both initial and relaxed structures exist; only the final DFT states should carry DFT-relaxed labels.

Phonon resources use different approximations: Petretto is ABINIT DFPT/PBEsol harmonic data; Kyoto/MDR harmonic calculations use VASP/phonopy; the larger MDR LTC release fits material-specific polynomial MLPs to DFT data; Phonix targets anharmonic interactions. The target pool, successful transport labels, displaced cells and materials are not interchangeable counts.

SuperBand combines experimental Tc with DFT electronic features. Its Scientific Data band cohort is 1,362 superconductors plus 1,112 verified negative examples; 2,474 is not a count of positive superconductors. JARVIS Supercon rows carry calculated phonon-mediated Tc, so their labels have different provenance.

Alex-MP-20 is a DFT-recomputed MP/Alexandria derivative, not MatterGen-generated output. The public 845,997-entry Alex-MP reference omits licensed ICSD components from the paper reference. OpenLAM Crystal Philately combines participant submissions and reused open repositories, all evaluated with MLIPs.

LeMat-Synth is included as adjacent synthesis/property context. Its 81k count means source papers and does not measure unique crystalline structures.

## Resources

- [Matbench v0.1 suite](https://matbench.materialsproject.org/): Thirteen property-prediction tasks from ten underlying sources; includes both composition-only and structure inputs. The catalogue groups shared structure cohorts and does not sum tasks as unique crystals.
- [Matbench Discovery data sets](https://matbench-discovery.materialsproject.org/data/sets): Registry of model training/reference/test datasets and a discovery-style stability benchmark. User-nominated page returned HTTP 403; its first-party GitHub registry and WBM documentation were used.
- [LeMat-GenBench](https://github.com/LeMaterial/lemat-genbench): Evaluates generated crystals using validity, distribution, diversity, novelty, and stability metrics with multi-MLIP/hull configurations; its reference datasets overlap LeMat-Bulk. Framework code is Apache licensed; not an additional crystal count.
- [MatterGen MP-20 reprocessed release](https://github.com/microsoft/mattergen/tree/main/data-release/mp-20): PBE-recomputed and element-filtered MP-20 derivative with its own distributed files; keep separate provenance from CDVAE MP-20. Exact file-row count was not verified, so not added as an independently sized dataset.
- [AFLOW AEL/AGL thermomechanical property libraries](https://aflow.org/documentation/): Distinct AFLOW workflows exposing elastic tensors/moduli and Debye/thermal estimates through AFLUX. Current property-bearing cohort sizes were not verified; no independent current materials count is claimed.

## Limits

- This slice prioritizes explicitly released property cohorts and generation benchmarks; broad parent databases are handled in other research slices.
- No summation across materials, structure entries, task samples, source papers, generated candidates or displaced configurations is valid.
- Some publisher/portal pages returned HTTP 403 or captcha; primary-paper text was retrieved as Europe PMC fullTextXML where available. Successful source responses and SHA-256 hashes are retained under research/evidence-benchmarks/.
- Dataset license is marked Not verified unless specific data terms were read; software licensing is not substituted for dataset licensing.
- Downloads of complete multi-terabyte and other large datasets were not performed. Most counts are author-reported release sizes; the PhononDB LTC count was independently derived from the author index.
- Full AFLOW AEL/AGL cohort size, raw LeMat-Synth access, and OpenLAM Crystal Philately bulk download remain unverified.
- No biological macromolecule/PDB or isolated-molecule corpus is included.
