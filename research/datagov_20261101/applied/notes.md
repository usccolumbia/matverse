# Applied-materials selection from the attached Data.gov list

Verified 2026-11-01. Local staging only; canonical datasets/resources, scripts, UI and Git state were not changed by this agent. Raw HTML, documents and payloads are confined to ignored `research/cache/datagov_20261101/applied/`. Public `receipts.json` preserves retrieval status, byte lengths and SHA-256 values. Checks support reuse/readiness signals; they do not independently certify numerical accuracy.

## Outcomes

- `additions.json`: one qualified small experimental-properties release, GDR thermoelectric materials (attached rank 99). The material workbook has **19 specimen rows**, not 19 unique compositions or crystal structures. Six p-type rows and 13 n-type rows include one earlier-work reference. The separate module workbook has 10 rows; these are never added to the material count. Density, geometric dimensions, resistivity and Seebeck units are present. Supporting methods identify alloying/hot pressing and ULVAC ZEM-3 testing. Exact stoichiometries, per-row temperatures, uncertainties, thermal conductivity and complete zT are absent. Several reported theoretical densities exceed 100%. The module workbook labels resistance as `mW`, a likely but uncorrected unit error. This is useful traceable development data, **not a high-quality reference-standard thermoelectric compilation**; integrating it should preserve those qualifications.
- `resources.json`: **11 adjacent engineering/polymer/textile resources**, outside any unique-crystal count. All correspond to literal attached titles; no broad unrelated discovery search was used. Strongest documentation/readiness signals are the elastomeric foam framework and uncertainty notes, the calibrated BLS/LFR packaging releases, NIR-SORT's dictionary and instrument/uncertainty structure, and the EAF slag leaching tables. Advanced metrology suite is catalog-level verification; full 2.5 GB payload was not inspected.
- `leads.json`: **22 attached rank dispositions**, preserving exact literal titles and mapping agency duplicates to one primary release.

## Retrieval caveats

Data.gov's older `/api/3/action/package_search` route returned 404. Search-page HTML supplied actual long-title truncated slugs; these were successfully fetched and crosswalked to primary identifiers. Initial guessed-slug 404 responses are retained as retrieval receipts and **do not indicate that the release is unavailable**. NIST landing-page fetch through web search timed out once; direct primary data payloads worked. Provenance errors above are observed source inconsistencies, not inferred reasons to merge releases.

NIST resources use the linked NIST non-SRD terms, not a fabricated blanket CC license. NIR-SORT's dataset-specific license explicitly permits worldwide royalty-free derivatives/distribution with acknowledgment and modified-data notices. EPA's inspected license states its default U.S. public-domain status unless specified otherwise. GDR datasets explicitly declare CC BY 4.0.
