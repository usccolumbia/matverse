# MatVerse research brief

Date: 2026-11-01. Audience: researchers choosing datasets for crystal materials informatics.

1. Which publicly documented datasets cover periodic crystal structures, atomistic configurations, experimental crystallography, and crystal-related properties?
2. How much data does each release contain, and what exactly is being counted: unique materials, structures, configurations, calculations, property records, or literature samples?
3. What differences in provenance, labels, access, licensing, and overlap affect reuse and fair benchmarking?

Scope includes inorganic bulk crystals, two-dimensional crystals, porous/framework crystals, molecular crystals, surfaces and interfaces, ML interatomic-potential datasets, and crystal-related property benchmarks. Isolated-molecule datasets are adjacent context only. Dataset portals and collection repositories are navigation resources, not additive datasets. No claim of exhaustive coverage; a living, source-verified survey.

Taxonomy assigns each dataset one primary category plus tags. Rankings descend within category by the documented numeric count; the counted unit and release must remain visible. Unknown counts are unranked. Never add heterogeneous counts into a total number of materials or imply DFT/generated structures are experimental discoveries.

Evidence: prioritize official releases, dataset cards, author repositories, data descriptors, and primary papers. Record verification date, count evidence, uncertainties, and source links. Retain source-response hashes where fetched. Research perspectives: broad inorganic/MLIP; experimental and literature data; domain-specific crystals and property benchmarks; access/counting/leakage audit.
