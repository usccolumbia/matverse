# MateriaViva source-table coverage audit

Checked 2026-11-02 against MatVerse dataset records at commit 3633858be178ef31bf7e9abfdcfc9dde9e667cae.

Of 38 source rows: **25 represented by independent dataset-family cards; 6 partially covered or represented by a different release; 7 missing as independent datasets.**

The [MateriaViva source table](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4QC/MateriaViva-Open#source-databases) reports structure membership within its aggregate, allowing a structure to count under multiple providers. These are not interchangeable with full provider counts, publication cohorts, configurations or archive entries. Its license labels are provider declarations for that source mapping, not an independent license audit.

“Present” confirms a dataset-family card, not that its release exactly reproduces the imported subset. “Partial” marks a specific repackage, historical release or grouped input that needs additional treatment. Upstream mentions inside MateriaViva property cards do not count as independent dataset cards.

No catalog entries or counts were changed during this audit.
