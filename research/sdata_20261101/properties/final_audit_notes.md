The **DSC paper/archive discrepancy is material**: the article states 41,680 devices, but `dsc_json.zip` contains 26,930 Elsevier plus 14,930 RSC JSONL documents, totaling 41,860, each with a distinct internal ID. The staging record uses the archive recount, calls the unit “device-report JSON documents,” and records the mismatch explicitly. The article's 475,045 DSC and 185,836 PSC totals count attribute sub-entries, not devices. For PSCs, the 8,259 + 7,559 archive documents match the stated 15,818 device records. Data include calculated/derived as well as directly extracted quantities; retain value-origin flags and irradiance/area assumptions.

The fabrication dataset contains 30 fields, including compositions, substrate/HTL/ETL/contact materials, architecture/flexibility and layer-deposition/spin-coating/annealing details. It supplies one source-article-linked record per DOI, rather than every distinct physical device in a paper. Phase I intentionally uses papers already represented in the manually curated Perovskite Database Project; Phase II extends the source corpus. It is a distinct automated extraction release, but its population cannot be added to that parent or the CDE corpus as disjoint unique materials/devices.

The emissivity dataset concerns engineered multilayers, cermets, composites, films and other designs. The downloaded archive has 153 JSONs and explicitly distinguishes experimental and calculated curves. Geometry keywords are not atomistic structures. Each curve carries source DOI/figure, axes units, method and extraction score. The source figures had 550 curves, and extraction recall was 27% over all curves and 55% over colored curves; recovered curves are a selected, digitized subset. This release differs from EKHI in source corpus, engineered-design metadata and inclusion of computed curves; no assertion of disjoint source materials is made.

The self-cleaning archive contains dictionaries, material-frequency statistics, numerical contact/sliding-angle and refractive/transmittance tables, and stability/durability sentiment summaries across six overlapping corpora. Inspected measurement files group each numeric value with a list of DOI/material pairs. Thus the number of top-level groups is neither unique materials nor individual measurements. The 39,011 figure is the source-paper count. A single global meaningful observation count remains unverified. Sentiment outputs are literature classifications rather than durability measurements. Polymers, composites and coatings are included, with no periodic coordinates.

All five records use `composition_only`: device component/geometry descriptions and composition strings do not establish periodic atomic structures. This is adjacent device/property/synthesis coverage, deliberately explicit about that boundary. Canonical schema and `url_ok` validation passed with 292 canonical records plus all five proposed additions. Raw publisher bodies and payloads remain in ignored `research/cache/sdata_properties/`; compact audit metadata, recounts, statuses and hashes remain in this staging directory.
