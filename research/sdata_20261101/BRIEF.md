The source is Scientific Data (ISSN 2052-4463), including legacy DOI syntax sdata.* and current s41597-*. A full Crossref journal cursor harvest retrieves title/DOI/date metadata indexed through the cutoff. A broad title-keyword pass supplies candidates; publisher and provider searches supplement keyword blind spots. Corrections/commentary are not new dataset releases. Retrieved papers are not automatically material datasets.

Independent primary-source perspectives: structures/computation/porous/low-dimensional releases; physical-property and synthesis datasets; characterization/processing/microstructure data. Existing 214 catalog records are checked before additions. Crystal-related experimental property or microstructure datasets without coordinates remain labeled; biological/geospatial-only datasets and isolated-molecule-only collections are adjacent leads rather than ranked crystal corpora.

Promotion requires verified primary dataset description and an explicit release/scope. Unverified counts remain null; an article license does not establish an external deposit license. Materials, temperature observations, frames, pathways, spectra and image tiles are not interchangeable or additive. Full payload downloads, scientific replication and exhaustive row-level quality audits are outside this documentation search.

The report must distinguish complete retrieval of the indexed journal metadata from completeness of indexing, keyword triage, and primary-source dataset verification. Corpus and title candidate ledgers, dispositions, source hashes, query URLs and unresolved leads must remain reviewable.
